Dispensina Digitale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Dispensina Digitale is an app for managing your home pantry inventory. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

No account, no server

Dispensina Digitale does not require account creation and has no backend server of its own. All data you enter (products, categories, shopping list, preferences) is stored exclusively on your device, locally. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared with the developer or any third party.

Camera

The app requests camera access only for the optional barcode scanning feature, used to speed up adding products. Camera images are processed entirely on-device (via Google ML Kit) and are never saved, transmitted, or uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable expiry notifications, the app schedules local notifications generated directly on your device to remind you when a product is about to expire. These notifications require no internet connection and use no third-party service (no Firebase Cloud Messaging).

Open Food Facts lookup

When you scan a barcode that isn't already in your local cache of previously entered products, and only if your device is online, the app may query the free, public Open Food Facts database to automatically retrieve the product name and details for that barcode. Only the scanned barcode number is sent in this request — no other personal or identifying data is transmitted. If you're offline or the product isn't found, you can still complete the entry manually.

Backup and data export

You can export a JSON backup file of your data at any time; it stays entirely under your control (save or share it as you wish). The app also automatically creates a silent internal safety backup on every launch, stored locally on the device and never uploaded anywhere.

Advertising and tracking

Dispensina Digitale contains no advertising, no profiling tools, no third-party analytics, and no advertising trackers.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data that could identify a minor, as it does not collect any personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

Any future changes to this policy will be published on this same page, with an updated revision date.
