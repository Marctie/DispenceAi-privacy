Dispensina Digitale Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Dispensina Digitale is a home pantry management app, available as an Android app and as a web app (which can also be installed on iPhone and iPad). This policy explains what data is processed, why, and how you can control it.

Controller and contact

The controller is the developer of Dispensina Digitale, reachable at: marco.p.develop@gmail.com.

Account

To use the app you create an account with an email address and password. The password is handled by the authentication service and is never visible to the developer. The part of your email before the "@" is used as your display name for other members of your pantry, if you share it.

Pantry data

The data you enter (products, quantities, expiry dates, optional prices, custom categories, shopping list, previously used barcodes) is stored on your device and, as a copy, in your online space, so you can access it from other devices and share it. Your preferences (theme, notification time and types, time zone) are stored in your profile and are not visible to other members.

Shared pantry

If you share your pantry with an invite code, the people who join can view and edit the products and the shopping list, and can see members' display names. The owner can remove a member or generate a new code; any member can leave at any time.

Notifications

In the Android app, expiry and low-stock notifications are generated and scheduled on the device. In the web app, if you enable notifications, your browser registers a push subscription (a technical address provided by the browser's push service, e.g. Apple, Google or Microsoft), which is stored in your profile and used only to send you the alerts you chose and alerts about products added by other members of your pantry. Notification content (e.g. product names) travels encrypted through the browser's push service.

Usage data and errors

To improve the app and fix problems, technical usage events are recorded without the content of your pantry (for example: signed in, product added, notifications enabled), together with the platform (Android, iPhone, web), app version, date, your username and a random installation identifier (generated by the app, not tied to hardware or advertising), as well as any app errors (technical message and the screen where they occurred). This data can only be accessed by the developer, who also receives it through a private notification channel (Telegram), and is kept for at most 12 months.

Data is always transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules prevent anyone from reading or editing another user's pantry unless they are a member of it.

Camera

In the Android app the camera is used only for optional barcode scanning. Images are processed exclusively on the device (Google ML Kit, on-device) and are never stored or transmitted.

Backup and export

You can export a JSON backup file at any time, which remains under your sole control. The app also keeps an internal safety backup on the device.

Legal basis and retention

Account and pantry data are processed to provide the service you requested and are kept as long as you keep your account. Usage and error data are processed on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure, and are kept for at most 12 months.

Your rights and account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, preferences and notification subscriptions are deleted; the pantry is deleted if it has no other members, otherwise it remains with the other members. If you cannot access the app you can request deletion by writing to marco.p.develop@gmail.com from your account's email address. You also have the right to access, rectify, restrict, object to and port your data (including via the backup export), and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Advertising

Dispensina Digitale contains no ads, does not sell data and does not use advertising trackers or third-party profiling tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with the update date.
